House rules

How we keep it friendly.

Four rules, no fine print. They keep the rooms useful for the person who is two weeks behind.

  • No spoilers

    Give the next step before the full solution. Let people finish the thought.

  • Ask the small question

    “This is probably dumb” is banned. Every question is on topic.

  • Explain, don’t perform

    Show the reasoning. The cleverest answer is the one someone can repeat.

  • Keep it human

    No put-downs, no homework dumps, no selling anything. Report, don’t escalate.
